LET’S FIND YOUR SAVE

A few clicks from your checklist.

For the Steam version on Windows:

  1. 1
    Open the game’s folder.

    In Steam, right-click Black Myth: Wukong → Manage → Browse local files.

  2. 2
    Follow this folder path.
    b1\Saved\SaveGames

    Open the folder named with your Steam ID.

  3. 3
    Choose the save you play.

    Look for ArchiveSaveFile.<slot>.sav. Choose the slot with the most recent modification time.

Keep your original save.

This tool only reads the file. It does not edit it or sync changes back to your game. Console saves are not supported.
